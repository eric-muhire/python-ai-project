# AI in my week

## Weekly Lunch Recommendation

Predicts what lunch option I am most likely to enjoy each week, from my previous meal choices, food preferences, budget, and eating habits.

When it is wrong, I may end up choosing food I do not enjoy, wasting money, or spending extra time deciding what to eat.

## Gym Exercise Planner

Predicts the best workout routine or exercises for me, from my fitness goals, workout history, performance, and activity patterns.

When it is wrong, I may follow an ineffective workout, make slower progress toward my fitness goals, or increase the risk of fatigue and injury.

## Fast Driving Route

Predicts the fastest route to my destination, from traffic conditions, road networks, travel history, GPS data, and estimated travel times.

When it is wrong, I may get delayed in traffic, take a longer route, use more fuel, and arrive later than planned.ll cost nothing.
